Our Story

Born From Love For Pets

The Happy Tails started in 2015 when our founder, a passionate veterinarian, noticed a gap in quality pet products. What began as a small corner shop has grown into a beloved destination for pet parents seeking premium, vet-approved products and services.

We believe every pet deserves the best. That's why we carefully curate our selection, partner with ethical suppliers, and employ staff who share our passion for animal welfare.
